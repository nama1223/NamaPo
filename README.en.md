## Overview

A training app that helps you "listen to and guess" and "tap along" to tempo. It's a game-like practice tool for developing a solid sense of tempo.

### Listening Training
Two methods for selecting the tempo of the sounded prompt: choose from options or input a numerical value. Limits include unlimited, survival (life system), and 60 seconds.

### Tap Training
"Self-Tap" where you tap after seeing the numerical value of the prompt's tempo; "Tempo Keep" where you listen to the example for 8 beats and then tap; and "Interference" mode, where after the example, a deliberately off-tempo beat keeps playing to throw you off.

### Other
Results are recorded in a point system and saved in your history. The prompt tempo list is editable. You can also set the type of sound and the proximity of the options (difficulty).

### Screen Explanation (How to read screenshots)
1) Mode Selection: Entry point to 6 training modes, history, and settings.
2) Listening: Use ▶ to listen for 8 beats and choose from 4 options.

3) Judgment: Points are added for correct answers, and consecutive correct answers are also displayed.

4) Self-Tap: Tap 8 times according to the prompt and judge your own timing.

5) Settings: Edit the tone, beat display method, difficulty level, and prompt list.
